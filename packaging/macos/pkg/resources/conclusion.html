Stuga is starting. Your browser opens its setup page as soon as Stuga runs; if it does not, choose Set Up Stuga… from the Stuga mark in the menu bar.

If your browser asks to find devices on local networks, choose Allow: that is how it reaches Stuga.

Once it is set up, other devices on your network open the address the menu shows. Show Address as QR Code… puts it on a phone.
